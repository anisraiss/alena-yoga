# Impeccable — Design Context

## Project

A redesign prototype for Alena Tikhonova, a Russian yoga teacher with 20+ years of experience. The current site is at yogatikhonova.com — built on GetCourse with a generic pastel wellness aesthetic that does NOT match her actual positioning as a serious movement educator who has trained with Ido Portal, studied Thomas Myers' Anatomy Trains, and coaches Russian celebrities including billionaire Oleg Tinkov.

## Target Audience

Russian-speaking adults, primarily 30–55, with disposable income and discerning taste. Many already know who Alena is through reputation or celebrity endorsements. They are NOT looking for "wellness vibes" — they want serious, science-based movement education from someone credible.

## Positioning

Premium. Quiet authority. The yoga teacher to billionaires. Science-backed, anatomically literate, no fluff. Think: if Aesop made a yoga school, or if Aman Resorts hired a movement educator.

## Aesthetic Direction

Editorial wellness meets anatomy lab. Premium, grounded, mature.

Reference points: Aman Resorts, Aesop, Goop on its more serious days, The Row, editorial fashion magazines covering wellness.

## Color Palette (strict — do not deviate)

- Background: Bone white `#F5F1EA`
- Primary accent: Deep terracotta `#A85B3A`
- Text / contrast: Forest charcoal `#2C342E`
- Secondary accent: Muted ochre `#C99757`
- Tertiary: Soft moss `#7A8B6F`

**NO** pastels. **NO** lavender. **NO** mint green. **NO** peach. **NO** pink.

## Typography

- **Display:** A serif with character and full Cyrillic support — preferred: Cormorant Garamond, Fraunces, or PT Serif. Large, confident, with generous letter-spacing on headlines.
- **Body:** A calm humanist sans with full Cyrillic — preferred: Inter Tight, Manrope, or PT Sans. NOT default Inter.
- Pair them with strong contrast in scale. Display should feel editorial; body should feel calm and readable.

## Motion

- Use the Motion library for React or vanilla JS animations.
- Subtle staggered text reveals on scroll.
- Slow image fades with light parallax.
- **NO** bouncing, **NO** wiggling, **NO** emoji animations, **NO** spinning text circles.
- Animations should feel like a magazine being read, not like a toy demo.

## Composition

- Mobile-first. Design for narrow screens first, then expand to desktop.
- Generous whitespace. Important things deserve room to breathe.
- Asymmetry over centered layouts where possible. Anchored to left or right, not always center.
- Large cinematic photography. Existing black-and-white photos of Alena should be kept and supported by warm-toned color framing — not floating against pastels.
- Typography that occasionally breaks the grid for emphasis.

## Language

The website content is in Russian. All copy should be kept in Russian, drawn directly from the existing site at yogatikhonova.com. Do not invent new Russian copy — we keep her voice authentic. UI elements (buttons, navigation) also in Russian.

## Anti-Patterns — NEVER do these

- No pastel backgrounds (no lavender, mint, peach, pink)
- No rounded organic blobs or amoeba shapes
- No rotating circle text around a profile photo
- No card-in-card layouts
- No emoji inside CTA buttons (no 🎁, no ✨)
- No testimonial modals — testimonials must be visible inline on the page
- No generic "AI slop" tells: no purple-to-blue gradients, no Inter font, no rounded square icon tiles above headings
- No "Welcome!" generic openings — the hero should make a real statement
- No three equally-weighted course cards — there should be visual hierarchy showing which course is the main offer

## Deliverable

A static HTML/CSS/JS prototype (single page or small set of files), built with **Vite + vanilla HTML/CSS/JS + Motion library**. Mobile-first responsive. Should open in any browser without a backend. Designed so it can be shown to Alena directly to convince her to commission a real redesign.
